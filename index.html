Electrical engineering

In progress

Switching maneuver design

Switching a feeder is a decision with a dozen constraints attached and no time to model them by hand. This pulls network topology, SCADA state and operations history into OpenDSS, then scores the candidate maneuvers by operational impact and by which constraints each one bends.

The scoring weights are the hard part, and they are still moving.

In progress

Protection coordination that keeps up

Relay setting groups tend to get chosen once and then left alone, even as the network around them keeps changing. I'm training models on grid topology and SCADA telemetry to predict which setting group an IED should actually be on, given the state the network is in right now rather than the state it was in at commissioning.

  • Topology data
  • ML
